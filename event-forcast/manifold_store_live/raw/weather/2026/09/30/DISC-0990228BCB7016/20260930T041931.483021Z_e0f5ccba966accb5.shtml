The Climate Prediction Center (CPC) provides climate forecasts to organizations that in turn disseminate the information to others.

  • The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) - Assessments of scientific information about climate change relevant for international and national policy formulation.

  • International Research Institute - Climate forecasts and assistance to other countries.

  • Pacific El-Niño-Southern Oscillation (ENSO) Applications Center - ENSO forecast for the Pacific Islands.

  • National Drought Mitigation Center - Climate forecasts for drought mitigation.

  • World Meteorological Organization - Global Weather and Climate forecasts.
