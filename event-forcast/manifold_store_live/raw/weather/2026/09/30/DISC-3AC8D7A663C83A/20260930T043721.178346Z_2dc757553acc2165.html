About NCEP.list.NOMADS-ftpprd
English (USA)

This subscription will be used by NCEP's Implementation and Data Services Branch to relay information solely pertaining to our data servers. This includes:

nomads.ncep.noaa.gov

NCEP will use the list to apprise subscribed users of any issues or notifications that pertain to any of our services. This could range from any server problem to data availability changes.

This list is not a forum for discussions and replies will not be distributed. If you have a question please email our team directly at:

ncep.pmb.dataflow@noaa.gov

Data flow to all the servers are supported operationally, but email inquiries will only receive a response during regular business hours (EST).
